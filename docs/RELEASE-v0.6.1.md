Smoother settings and drawer, and a badge color fix.

### Fixed

- Turning the notification badge off and on again no longer flashes **System** before your chosen badge color (for example **Red**) is selected.

### Changed

- Settings pages now slide in and out inside one panel.
- Applying a theme is now a slide to confirm, so a stray tap does not change the theme.
- Switches in settings stretch as they move.
- The Personal and Work tabs in the app drawer stretch as you switch between them.
- The initial-letter popup in the app drawer unfolds from the side, and its rows appear faster.

### Install

Download `QSearchLauncher-0.6.1.apk` below and install it over 0.6.0. Your settings stay.

This release adds no new permissions. The permission list is in [docs/PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/wuugi/qsearch-launcher/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md) (Korean).
