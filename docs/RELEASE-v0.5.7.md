More room for widgets on square screens.

### Change

- On square and near-square screens (like the 1440x1440 Titan 2), the favorites now use less height. Two rows take at most about a third of the screen, so widgets get more of it. Favorite icons are a little smaller there. Phones with taller screens look the same as before.
- If all your favorites fit in one row, only one row of space is kept for them. The space for a second row now goes to widgets. When you add a fifth favorite, the second row comes back and the icons keep their size.
- When widgets fill their space, they now go all the way down to the favorites and fade out there, instead of fading out early and leaving an empty strip. That bottom strip still works as empty space even with a widget under it: long-press it to add a widget, double-tap it, or swipe up from it to open the app drawer.

### Install

Download `QSearchLauncher-0.5.7.apk` below and install it over 0.5.6. Your settings stay.

This release adds no new permissions. The permission list is in [docs/PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/wuugi/qsearch-launcher/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md) (Korean).
